02

Local storage — what stays on your device

The following are created and stored solely on your Android device (using a local Room database and SharedPreferences). None of it is transmitted to any server we operate:

  • Favourite stops — the stops you star for quick access
  • Departure alerts — which lines you asked to be alerted about
  • Cached transit data (GTFS) — stops, routes, trips and timetables for Bucharest, downloaded once and refreshed periodically
  • App settings & state — including an anonymous per-install identifier used only to talk to the live transit API, and a flag that you accepted the terms and gave consent on first launch
  • Pro status — whether you've unlocked TransitLive Pro, cached locally after Google Play confirms your purchase
ℹ️
GPS location When you grant location permission, the app reads your GPS position to find stops within walking distance and to center the map on you. This is used in real time on your device and is not logged or transmitted anywhere by us.
03

Live transit data

To show real-time vehicles and arrivals, TransitLive makes standard HTTPS requests to public Bucharest public-transport data services (operated by STB and TPBI) and downloads the static GTFS schedule. As with any web request, those servers receive your device's IP address in order to respond. We send only what is needed to fetch transit data — no personal identifiers, no location coordinates.

Map imagery is loaded from OpenStreetMap / CARTO tile servers when you view the map; those servers likewise receive a standard HTTP request including your IP address.

04

Advertising & AdMob

TransitLive is free and supported by ads delivered by Google AdMob — native ads shown in the nearby-departures list and in the vehicle/stop detail sheets, plus an interstitial ad shown when you exit the app.

GDPR consent

On first launch (and whenever legally required), a GDPR consent form appears — powered by Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). You can:

  • Accept personalized ads — AdMob may use your advertising ID and device info to show more relevant ads.
  • Decline / use non-personalized ads — ads are still shown, but without personalization. AdMob still collects some contextual signals (e.g. approximate IP-based location, app context).

Where required, the consent form is shown to users in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland; users elsewhere may not see it.

What AdMob may collect (if you consented)

  • Your Google advertising ID
  • Device model, OS version, mobile network
  • Approximate location (IP-based)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

For full details, see the Google Privacy Policy and AdMob data practices.

4B

Analytics & crash reporting

TransitLive uses Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics (both Google services) to catch crashes and understand basic, aggregate app usage — for example, which screens are opened or whether the app crashed on your device. This runs independently of the AdMob consent choice described above; it is not used for advertising and is not linked to your identity.

Crashlytics reporting is disabled entirely in development/debug builds and only active in the version you install from Google Play. Data collected includes device model, OS version, app version, and stack traces at the time of a crash — never the contents of your favourites, alerts, or location history.

For full details, see the Firebase privacy and security policy.

4C

Purchases & subscriptions

TransitLive offers an optional TransitLive Pro upgrade (a monthly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase) that removes ads and unlocks a Pro app icon. All purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing — we never see or store your payment details (card numbers, billing address, etc.). We only receive confirmation of your entitlement (i.e. that you own "Pro"), which is used on-device to disable ads and switch the launcher icon.

Subscriptions can be managed or cancelled at any time from the Google Play Store app under Payments & subscriptions.

05

Permissions the app requests

  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION — optional; used to find nearby stops and center the map. Granted and revocable by you at any time in Android Settings.
  • INTERNET — required to load OSM map tiles, fetch live transit data, and show AdMob ads.
  • AD_ID — required by Google Play policy when AdMob is present; used by AdMob only if you consent.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+) — optional; requested only for departure alerts you arm yourself.

Third-party integrations

  • Google AdMob — ad delivery and GDPR consent management (after consent).
  • Firebase Crashlytics & Analytics — crash reporting and aggregate usage stats.
  • Google Play Billing — processing TransitLive Pro purchases and subscriptions.
  • STB / TPBI transit services — real-time vehicle positions, arrivals and the static GTFS schedule.
  • OpenStreetMap / CARTO — map tile imagery, loaded when you view the map.
07

Data sharing

We do not sell, trade or rent your personal information to anyone. The only data that leaves your device is:

  • Ad-related signals sent to Google AdMob (only after consent)
  • Crash and usage data sent to Firebase Crashlytics / Analytics
  • Purchase requests sent to Google Play Billing (if you choose to buy TransitLive Pro)
  • Standard HTTPS requests to STB / TPBI transit servers and OSM / CARTO tile servers (standard IP logging by those services)
08

Your rights & controls

  • Access & deletion — all your locally stored data is on your device; clear it via Android Settings → Apps → TransitLive → Clear data, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Location — revoke any time via Android Settings → Apps → TransitLive → Permissions.
  • Ad consent — you can reset or change your consent choice from within the app; declining stops personalized ads.
  • Analytics/Crashlytics deletion request — email us (below) with your approximate install date and device model and we will submit a deletion request to Firebase for any associated crash/usage data.

Children's privacy
