AMethod fits the work

No single framework, forced onto everything.

LuXTech does not push every initiative into Scrum, or into any one method. The right delivery model depends on the work. We use Waterfall, Agile, Scrum, Kanban, SAFe, PM², PRINCE2 or a pragmatic hybrid when each is appropriate.

Waterfall Agile Scrum Kanban SAFe PM² PRINCE2 Hybrid

The method sets the structure; the tooling fits how your team already works. We run delivery with a wide array of tools, Mural, Klaxoon, Confluence and others, rather than forcing a single stack on every engagement.

BWhere we fit

You may need us when…

  • A delayed or drifting project needs someone to recover control of scope, time and budget.
  • A complex infrastructure or transformation programme needs senior, hands-on leadership.
  • Delivery is stuck in ceremonies that don’t fit, and the method needs an honest reassessment.
  • A PMO or governance layer needs designing, or improving so reporting is actually useful.
  • Multiple concurrent projects need coordinating across teams, vendors and dependencies.
  • You need interim project leadership to hold delivery together through a transition.
