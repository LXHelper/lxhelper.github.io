The essentials TransitPulse keeps your data on your device. It uses mobile/Wi-Fi data to load the map, fetch live transit positions, and show ads. Nothing personal is collected by us — the only outside data processing is Google AdMob, and only after you consent.
01

Network & data consumption

The app uses your internet connection for:

  • Map tiles — downloaded from OpenStreetMap / CARTO as you pan and zoom. This is the largest data user; cached by the OS where possible.
  • Live transit data — small, frequent requests (roughly every 10 seconds while the app is open) for vehicle positions and arrivals.
  • GTFS schedule download — a one-time download (~8 MB) of the Bucharest schedule, refreshed periodically.
  • Ads — AdMob loads ad creatives over the network.

If you are on a limited data plan, most usage comes from map tiles; staying zoomed on one area reduces it.

03

Data retention

Locally stored data (favourites, alerts, cached schedule, settings) persists until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. We hold no server-side copy of your data. Data processed by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's policies.

04

Security

All network requests use HTTPS. Data stored on your device is protected by Android's app sandbox. Because we operate no backend that stores your personal data, there is no central database of user data to breach.
